# Negotiation rules (template — fill in your own)

These are the rules the agent applies autonomously when negotiating editorial terms. Anything outside the rules escalates to Tier 3.

## Compliance baseline (NEVER autonomous)
- Anything involving compensation must have explicit `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` agreed in writing before placement. The agent enforces this in outreach copy and verifies on the live link.
- If a publisher pushes back on `rel` attribution, the agent escalates — humans decide whether to walk away.

## Within autonomous range (agent decides)
- Turnaround timelines (e.g. "we can get you a draft within X days").
- Content fit suggestions (different angle, different target page if it serves the publisher's audience better).
- Volume context: agent can mention "we run a steady editorial calendar" to anchor relationship vs. one-off.
- Partnership context: agent can offer ongoing collaboration where appropriate.
- Walk-away: if publisher's terms don't fit, agent walks politely.

## Outside autonomous range (escalate)
- Any thread where compensation is being discussed — humans handle, even if your business model includes paid placements.
- Publisher demands editorial control or pre-publication review of the article (relationship judgement, escalate).
- Publisher requests payment up front before draft is even shared (high fraud risk, escalate).
- Publisher offers something other than what was asked (different page, different anchor type) — escalate to confirm intent.

## Communication tone
- Always polite. Always professional.
- Never haggle more than once per thread (one counter, then accept-or-walk).
- Never imply pressure or urgency unless it's true.
- Always reference the prior message context — show you read what they wrote.

## Specific phrasings to AVOID
- "best price" / "lowest price" — sounds transactional.
- "as agreed" — when nothing was agreed.
- "this is our final offer" — not until it actually is.
- Anything that implies a script or template — publishers detect this immediately.

## Phrasings to USE
- "Given we'd be placing [N] this month across our roster..."
- "Could we make this work at [X] for the first link, with a view to ongoing?"
- "Totally understand if [X] doesn't work for you — appreciate the time."
