# Vessa deployment

First use of the influencer-avatars set. Vessa is a stretching app Adrian
is building in Claude Code on his PC. Its exercise illustrations are already
animated through Higgsfield via the `exercise-animation` skill, so the model
choice, prompt template and 9:16 rules for movement footage are settled.

This folder holds what is specific to Vessa. The general procedure is in
`../pipeline.md`.

## Open decisions (Adrian's)

1. **What the funnel points at.** The app itself (store listing), a 30-day
   stretch challenge as a paid workbook, or the workbook free as the lead
   magnet into the app. The source's argument for a workbook is that it is
   structured and interactive, which a free chatbot answer is not; the same
   argument applies to an app, more strongly.
2. **How many personas at launch.** The source ran three and the early
   loser went viral. Two or three is the minimum for the comparison to mean
   anything.
3. **Which platforms from day one.** The recommendation in `../README.md`
   is all three (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).

## Persona candidates

To be generated at step 2. The angle rule: the opposite of the niche's
default presenter, who is young, gym-based, and already flexible. Contrast
directions worth trying, one persona each:

- the older person who moves better than people half their age, and says
  how
- the desk worker who fixed a specific problem (stiff hips, tight neck)
  with ten minutes a day, and reports rather than preaches
- the coach voice: dry, precise, one cue per video

Each candidate is a folder under `../personas/` when generated. The
health-claims check in the persona template is mandatory for all of them.

## What exists already

- `exercise-animation` skill: validated Higgsfield settings for Vessa
  movement clips, including the format plan for reclined and lying
  exercises. Any B-roll that shows the avatar performing a stretch should
  use those settings.
- Higgsfield and HeyGen connectors on the Claude account.
