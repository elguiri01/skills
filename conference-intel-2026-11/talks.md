## Not turned into a skill, and why

- **Building company agents (Borders).** A vendor's architecture with no
  results. Two ideas are sound and already ours in other words: access
  decisions belong in a policy layer outside the model (our sentinels: "obedience
  is not a control, structure is"), and the model should never see a credential
  (our off-limits rule). His twelve-question test is at line 977 if a client
  asks what a "company agent" is.
- **One book to 660 pieces (Olsina).** Volume from one source is the opposite
  of the information-gain gate unless the source is itself unique data, and her
  figures have no baselines.
- **Kimi, Chinese market.** In `plays.md` under "watch".
- **Dennis Yu's agent workflow.** One rule worth holding: the model that does
  the work is never the one that checks it.
