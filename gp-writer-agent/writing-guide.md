# Editorial guest post writing guide

Drop this in your skill dir. The agent reads it before every draft.

## Voice — what to do
- Editorial journalism. Magazine-quality prose.
- One concrete fact, stat, or example per ~300 words. Pulled from research blob only.
- Specific over generic. Names of brands, tools, frameworks where relevant. NOT names of individual people unless famous public figures.
- A point of view, not a hedge. Pick a position and defend it.
- Short sentences mixed with longer ones. Variation creates rhythm.
- One-line lead that hooks, then 2-3 sentence intro that doesn't restate the title.

## Voice — what NOT to do
- "In this article we will explore..." — instant tell.
- "In conclusion..." — boilerplate.
- Personal anecdotes ("I tested this for six weeks..."). Unless explicitly enabled per client.
- First-person ("I", "we", "our") unless brand voice requires it.
- Marketing copy ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "best-in-class").
- Made-up stats. EVER. If it's not in the research blob, don't cite it.
- Listicle padding ("here are 7 ways to..."). Use lists only when the content is genuinely list-shaped.

## SEO discipline
- Hit target keyword in first 100 words, in one H2, in one paragraph organically.
- Use NLP/entity terms naturally — don't stuff them.
- Quality floor: optimisation tool score 75+. Don't chase higher at the expense of readability.
- Anchor text: place the target anchor once in body, in a paragraph that genuinely fits.
- Internal linking: 1-2 links to other pages on the publisher's site. Out of basic respect.

## Word count
- Stay within 5% of the target word count.
- Over-shoot is the most common failure. If the draft runs long, cut from the middle (sub-section padding), not from the lead or conclusion.

## Banned phrases
- "It is important to note that..."
- "It goes without saying that..."
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Without further ado..."

## When to abort
- Optimisation tool unreachable.
- Research blob is empty / Perplexity/Google failed.
- Client niche is one of the banned-topics list for the publisher (check brief).
- Target URL returns 404.

Abort cleanly: log reason, post to team channel, move on. NEVER guess.
